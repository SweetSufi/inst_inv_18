What might Philadelphia preserve across four centuries of change? This scene keeps familiar civic forms in view while imagining a quieter, wintertime view of the Schuylkill River and new architecture. Select a marker to inspect the difference between an identifiable present-day landmark and a possible future.

Behind the Investigation:
Research Note

Behind the Investigation explains the generative AI capabilities, technologies, and design decisions demonstrated by each Institute investigation.

Prompt Method Used

One-shot AI-assisted microsite development using an approved speculative cityscape, historical reference constraints, and the Institute's typography and research standards.

Skill Demonstrated

Historical Synthesis & Visual Storytelling

Connecting identifiable contemporary landmarks to an explicitly speculative future environment.

Research Question

How can an interactive visualization of a future city distinguish recognizable civic heritage from imagined technological and architectural change?

How It Works

Visitors select unobtrusive markers on a static approved illustration to read contextual notes about Philadelphia landmarks and speculative urban design.

What This Demonstrates

The ability to organize factual geographic anchors and creative future scenarios into a legible, exploratory interface.

Technical Implementation

The visual was created with AI assistance. The website uses local image assets, HTML, CSS, and deterministic JavaScript. There is no live LLM or on-demand image generation during exploration.

Evidence and Limitations

The year 2400 is fictional. No architectural, transportation, preservation, environmental, or climate predictions are presented as facts. The image is an artistic composite: its relative positions and sightlines do not establish geographic accuracy. The familiar landmarks are interpretive visual references rather than verified future structures.
