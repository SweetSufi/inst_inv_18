A city of overlapping eras

Tokyo in 2010 combined dense contemporary infrastructure with neighborhoods, religious architecture, traditional craft, and older street forms. This imagined winter evening brings several of those visual layers together into one quiet scene.

Institution

The Institute of Unnecessary Research

Investigation

18 — Meanwhile, in 1967... / Tokyo, 2010

Primary Capability

Historical Synthesis and Visual Storytelling

Research Question

How can an AI-assisted visual environment communicate the coexistence of historical and contemporary architecture in Tokyo without presenting a composite image as documentary evidence?

Method

One approved visual guideline and an AI-assisted single-page interactive microsite, with five contextual markers and restrained ambient light animation.

How It Works

Visitors select markers placed over the illustration to read short, authored contextual interpretations. The selected information is displayed using deterministic JavaScript, with keyboard-accessible controls and a close action.

Technical Implementation

Static HTML, CSS, local fonts, local AI-generated image, and rule-based JavaScript. There is no live LLM inference, external API, or dynamic historical generation at runtime.

What This Demonstrates

AI-assisted environmental storytelling, cross-era visual synthesis, and accessible contextual interpretation.

Historical and Visual Limitations

Tokyo Tower opened in 1958 and stood in 2010. Tokyo Skytree was under construction in 2010 and opened in 2012; it is not represented here. The pagoda, traditional lane, tower sightline, lanterns, and surrounding high-rises form an artistic composite and should not be read as an exact real-world viewpoint. The image's snowfall and street appearance depict an imagined evening, not verified weather or a dated photograph. Shopfront iconography is illustrative.

Research Starting Points

Tokyo Tower official website · Tokyo Metropolitan Government tourism guide · Tokyo Skytree official website. These are research starting points, not verification of the composite scene.
