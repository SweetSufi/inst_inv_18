Behind the Investigation:
Research Note

Behind the Investigation explains the generative AI capabilities, technologies, and design decisions demonstrated by each Institute investigation.

Prompt Method Used

Grounded prompting: the historical narrative is constrained by referenced scholarship, while uncertain visual details are identified as interpretive. The microsite was developed with AI assistance using the approved visual direction and the Institute’s typography and documentation standards.

Skill Demonstrated

Historical Synthesis & Visual Storytelling

This investigation demonstrates how historical research, visual reconstruction, and interactive object interpretation can be combined while distinguishing documented context from artistic invention.

Research Question

How can an interactive historical environment distinguish documented period context from artistic reconstruction?

How It Works

Visitors examine an imagined household in Chang’an around 750 by selecting objects and the exterior view. Each selection opens a concise interpretation, identifies uncertainty, and provides links to relevant historical sources.

What This Demonstrates

A visually led, evidence-conscious approach to historical storytelling that encourages visitors to explore material culture without treating a generated image as a historical record.

Technical Implementation

The scene is static AI-generated artwork. Object hotspots, navigation, and explanatory panels use deterministic browser JavaScript and predefined text. No live LLM or real-time historical image generation runs during visitor exploration.

Historical Limitations

The room, furnishings, arrangement, landscape scroll, and exterior skyline have not been individually authenticated for a Chang’an household in 750. The scene is an interpretive concept, not a documented reconstruction. Linked sources support broader historical context rather than certifying each pictured object.

Representation Audit

Established: The experience identifies a particular city and period, separates contextual evidence from artistic interpretation, and does not present a funerary ceramic as a verified everyday tea service.

Outstanding: Specialist review of architecture, furniture, textiles, vessel forms, the low table setting, flowering branches, and the prominent pagoda is required before any claim of historical accuracy.
